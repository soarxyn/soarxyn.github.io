I am a post-masters ML researcher working with [Johannes Stegmaier](https://www.hhu.de/jun-prof-dr-johannes-stegmaier-zum-professor-fuer-machine-learning-for-medical-data-an-der-hhu-ernannt) at [HHU Düsseldorf](https://www.hhu.de/en/) on generative models and ML for medical data. Alongside this, I've been implementing foundational generative architectures (DDPM, DiT, VQGAN, autoregressive LLMs) which I write about in my blog.

I earned my MSc. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at [RWTH Aachen](https://www.rwth-aachen.de/) through the [T.I.M.E. Double Degree program](https://timeassociation.org/), where I've worked with Prof. [Stegmaier](https://www.hhu.de/jun-prof-dr-johannes-stegmaier-zum-professor-fuer-machine-learning-for-medical-data-an-der-hhu-ernannt) on automated microscopy and Chest X-Ray VLMs. I've also completed my BSc. at the [University of São Paulo](https://www5.usp.br/), graduating first in my class. During my undergraduate years, I contributed to a clinical trial on a digital intervention for late-life depression in Brazil, led by [Marcia Scazufca](https://scholar.google.com.br/citations?user=x7mQBgYAAAAJ), and worked with Professor [Edson Gomi](https://www2.pcs.usp.br/~gomi/) on model interpretability.

Outside academia, I've worked as an ML engineer at [BTG Pactual](https://www.btgpactual.com/) on autonomous agent systems and model monitoring, and earlier as an ML intern at [QI Tech](https://qitech.com.br/) on biometric anti-spoofing and as a software intern at [Robert Bosch](https://www.bosch.com/).

When I'm not working, you'll likely find me exploring new coffee shops, cooking, or playing World of Warcraft.
